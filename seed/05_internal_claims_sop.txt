STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SOP-CLM-014
Claims Adjudication Workflow - New-Member Triage

Owner: Claims Operations Department
Effective Date: June 1, 2025
Document Version: 2.4

1. OVERVIEW

This SOP describes the claims adjudication workflow for medical claims received for members within their first 90 days of enrollment with Acme Health Plan. This SOP applies to professional, outpatient institutional, and ambulatory surgical center claims.

2. PROCESS STEPS

Step 1: Claim Intake
The claim is received via EDI 837 transaction or paper submission. The claim is loaded into the core claims engine and assigned a claim control number.

Step 2: Member Eligibility Verification
The claims engine verifies member eligibility for the date of service. Claims for non-eligible members are rejected with the appropriate denial code.

Step 3: Provider Validation
The rendering and billing providers are validated against the network roster. Out-of-network providers are routed to the out-of-network adjudication queue.

Step 4: Prior Authorization Check
The claims engine queries the internal PA module to determine whether a prior authorization on file matches the claimed service. Where no internal PA is found, the claim is held for medical necessity review by clinical staff.

Step 5: Benefit Application
The claims engine applies the member's benefit package, including deductibles, copayments, coinsurance, and out-of-pocket maximums.

Step 6: Final Adjudication
The claims engine produces a payment determination, generates the EOB, and routes the claim to disbursement.

3. SPECIAL HANDLING

3.1 New-Member Claims

For claims with a service date within the member's first 90 days of enrollment, no special handling is performed under this current SOP version. Claims are adjudicated under standard logic.

3.2 Pended Claims

Claims pended for medical necessity review are routed to the clinical review queue with a target turnaround time of 14 calendar days.

4. SYSTEMS INVOLVED

- Core Claims Engine (vendor: Cognizant TriZetto Facets)
- Prior Authorization Module (internal: PA-MOD-v4)
- Provider Roster (internal: PROV-DB)
- Member Eligibility System (internal: MEM-ELIG)

5. METRICS

This SOP is monitored against the following service-level metrics:

- Average claim turnaround time: target ≤ 14 calendar days
- First-pass adjudication rate: target ≥ 92%
- Claim rework rate: target ≤ 4%
