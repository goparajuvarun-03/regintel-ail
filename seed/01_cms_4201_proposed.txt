CMS-SAMPLE-4201-P (Proposed Rule)

Medicare Advantage and Part D Programs:
Continuity of Care Protections During Plan Transitions

Issuing Body: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services
Effective Date: January 15, 2026
Status: Proposed Rule

SUMMARY

This proposed rule would establish new continuity-of-care protections for Medicare Advantage (MA) members who transition between plans. The rule would require MA organizations to honor prior authorization decisions issued by a member's previous plan for a transition period, ensuring members do not experience disruption in ongoing courses of treatment.

§ 422.138 Continuity of Care During Plan Transitions

(a) Scope. This section applies to all Medicare Advantage organizations and all members who transition between MA plans, including transitions from Original Medicare to MA, and transitions between MA organizations.

(b) Prior Authorization Honor Period. A Medicare Advantage organization shall honor any active prior authorization decision issued by a member's previous plan for a period of not less than 30 days following the member's effective date of enrollment.

(c) Documentation. The MA organization shall document its determination to honor or extend the prior authorization in the member's case file and notify the treating provider in writing within 7 business days of the member's effective enrollment date.

(d) Member Communication. Members shall be notified of their continuity-of-care rights through the standard enrollment materials and through the plan's website.

§ 422.166 Quality Rating Adjustments

(a) Star Rating Calculation. The Star Rating calculation methodology shall remain unchanged for measurement year 2026.

(b) Guardrails. Cut points for individual measures shall be capped at 1 standard deviation from the prior year's cut points.

§ 422.504 Compliance Requirements

(a) Internal Policy Updates. MA organizations shall update internal policies, procedures, and member communications to reflect the requirements of § 422.138 no later than the effective date.

(b) Audit Documentation. MA organizations shall maintain documentation sufficient to demonstrate compliance with § 422.138 for a period of not less than 10 years.

(c) Training. Customer service representatives, claims processors, and prior authorization staff shall receive training on continuity-of-care requirements no later than the effective date.
